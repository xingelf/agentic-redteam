What a Breach Costs the Share Price

When a public company discloses a major cyber incident, does the market punish it — and for how long? This is an event study: for each incident we model the stock's normal relationship to the market, then measure the abnormal return around the disclosure date, so a general market rally or sell-off is stripped out. The line that matters is the cumulative abnormal return (CAR).

Method: market-model event study. For each incident, AR = Rstock − (α + β·Rmarket), with α and β estimated over a pre-event window; CAR is the running sum. The average line is the cumulative average abnormal return (CAAR). Benchmark: S&P 500.

Prices: public market data API (adjusted close). Disclosure dates: public filings and reporting. No client, target, or engagement data. Not investment advice.
